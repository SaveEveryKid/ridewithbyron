# Google Ads build sheet — RideWithByron.com

One Search campaign, five ad groups, about 30 minutes to enter. Sized for **$15 a day**.

Before any ad runs: get the dealership's okay to advertise their inventory, and ask how they want the Polaris name and prices handled in ads.

---

## Step 1 — Create the account

1. Go to **ads.google.com** and sign in with the Google account you want to own this.
2. If Google offers a guided "Smart campaign" setup, look for **Switch to Expert Mode** (small link near the bottom) and use that. The guided setup spends money on placements you don't want.
3. Enter billing when asked.

## Step 2 — Campaign settings

Create a new campaign and choose these:

| Setting | Choose |
|---|---|
| Goal | **Leads** (or "Create a campaign without a goal's guidance") |
| Campaign type | **Search** |
| Campaign name | `RideWithByron - Search - Shoals` |
| Networks | **Untick** "Search partners" and **untick** "Display Network" |
| Locations | **Enter another location → Advanced search → Radius**: 50 miles around **Florence, Alabama** |
| Location options | **Presence: People in or regularly in your included locations** |
| Languages | English |
| Budget | **$15.00** per day |
| Bidding | **Maximize clicks**, and set a **maximum cost-per-click bid limit of $2.00** |
| Ad schedule | All days, 6:00 AM – 10:00 PM |
| AI Max / "Broad match keywords" campaign setting | **Off** |
| Automatically created assets | **Off** |

Why "Maximize clicks" to start: Google needs about 15–30 leads recorded before its lead-based bidding works well. Switch to "Maximize conversions" after that.

## Step 3 — Ad groups, keywords and ads

Enter keywords exactly as written, **including the quote marks or square brackets**. Quotes mean "this phrase must be in the search"; brackets mean "this exact search". Without them Google matches loosely and wastes budget.

For each ad group, create one **Responsive search ad**: paste the Final URL, then the headlines and descriptions.

## Step 5 — Add-ons to the ads (assets)

In **Assets**, add these so the ads take up more room:

- **Sitelinks:** `Shop All Inventory` → `/inventory` · `New Units` → `/inventory?condition=New` · `Pre-Owned` → `/inventory?condition=Used` · `About Byron` → `/about` (put `https://www.ridewithbyron.com` in front of each)
- **Callouts:** `No Dealer Fees` · `Upfront Pricing` · `Real Photos` · `Deal With One Person`
- **Call asset:** your phone number (calls from ads go straight to you)

Leave out **Location assets** unless the dealership agrees: those would show the store's Google listing under your ad.

## Step 6 — Lead tracking (so you know which ads work)

Already working with no setup: when someone clicks an ad and then sends a form on the site, the lead shows up in your site dashboard and in Torque with the source **Google Ads**.

To also show leads inside Google Ads (needed for Google to optimize toward leads):

1. In Google Ads: **Goals → Conversions → New conversion action → Website**.
2. Enter `ridewithbyron.com`, then choose to **add a conversion action manually**.
3. Category **Submit lead form**, name `Website lead`, value "Don't use a value", count **One**.
4. Choose **Use Google Tag Manager / install the tag yourself**. Google will show two codes:
   - a **Conversion ID** that looks like `AW-1234567890`
   - a **Conversion label**, a string of letters and numbers
5. Send both to Claude. They get added to the site and tracking is live.

One limit to know: visitors are asked to allow tracking, and the Google tag loads only if they say yes. Google Ads will therefore show fewer leads than you really got. Your site dashboard and Torque show all of them.

## Step 7 — The first two weeks

- **Day 2–3:** open **Insights and reports → Search terms**. This shows what people really typed. Add anything irrelevant to the negative list.
- **Weekly:** check which ad group brings leads. Move budget toward it.
- **If clicks cost more than $2 and you're getting few:** raise the bid limit to $3.
- **After about 15–30 leads:** change bidding to **Maximize conversions**.

A realistic expectation at $15 a day: roughly 8–15 clicks a day. How many become leads depends on the units and prices, so give it two to three weeks before judging.
